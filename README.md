## 🛡️ A Knight's Tale

**A Knight's Tale** is a 2001 medieval adventure comedy-film written and directed by Brian Helgeland. It tells the story of William Thatcher, a peasant squire who poses as a knight to compete in jousting tournaments, finding glory, romance, and friendship along the way.

* **Famous Quote:** *"Your men love you. If I knew nothing else about you, that would be enough."*
